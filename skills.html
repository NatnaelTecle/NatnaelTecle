Alteryx

Data preparation, reconciliation, and business analysis

I completed an individual data consulting project for Eddie's Tennis Warehouse, a wholesale tennis equipment distributor, covering a full year of its sales-to-cash process. I loaded multiple files (invoices, customers, cash receipts, product list, and sales team data), cleansed them, and tied each one to the client's control totals before any analysis, so the results could be trusted.

I then built workflows to examine management's concerns, including whether the reported accounts receivable balance of $590,738.64 was supportable, whether customers stayed within credit limits, which invoices were more than 90 days old and might need write-offs, whether early-payment discounts followed policy, whether sales reps used official retail prices, and what commissions would cost. For each result, I wrote up what it meant for the business, not just the numbers, in a client-ready report.
